About me

I’m MARCO.

After going through a period of anxiety, I wanted to understand what was happening to me. I studied hypnotherapy and ways to work with emotions, thoughts and body sensations. Today I help others explore their experiences and practice tools they can use in daily life.

My approach

Listen first. Practice together.

In each session, we talk about what you are going through, choose tools that make sense for you and put them into practice. I draw on different approaches based on your goals.
